A profile now offers recorded weeks in each of the seven roles, the first assignment, and the longest unchanged role-and-target span. Source controls open the real starting assignment and the following change or death record. It adds no simulation, save, profession or relationship rule and leaves `resident-historian-v2` unchanged.

## What a recorded week means

A `work_changed` row identifies the actual assigned person and the role/target that applies in that week. The next assignment starts a new span and closes the old one in the preceding week. Assignments happen before demographics, so the death week remains included and a newborn first receives an assignment in the following week. The final span ends at the person's recorded death week, or the current world week for a living person. Deceased histories remain frozen when playback continues. Founders have no assignment at week zero and earlier work is unknown.

Role totals sum these recorded weeks. Resting includes children, seniors and workers without a productive assignment. An assigned role does not establish completed output, skill, achievement, feelings or a permanent profession. A target change starts a new span even when the role stays the same. Tied longest spans use the earliest change record. The existing personal timeline retains all individual changes; the summary renders seven totals and a bounded pair of featured spans.

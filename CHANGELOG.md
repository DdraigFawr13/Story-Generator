# Changelog

Newest first. One short entry per merged milestone.

## Phase 2 — Peoples and Legends

- **M2.2 Settlement and sites.** A new `settle` world-gen stage (`engine/history/settle.ts`, rules in `content/settlement.ts`) founds 3–8 peoples (about one per 3,500 land cells), each with a capital and, half the time, a second site picked by the culture's preferred biomes and site preferences (river, coast, aura, plains, deposit, forest, heights), fertility and nearby route nodes. Sites have a kind (hamlet to city, fortress, mine, temple, port), a carrying capacity from fertility, fish and game within 3 cells with port, ford and road bonuses, a population in child, adult and elder bands by race, stores and the features that drew them. Territory is the nearest site by travel cost. Founding emits `civ_founded` and `site_founded` events with cause links. The viewer gains a Peoples layer (key 0), a Sites overlay (key S, glyph by kind, size by population, names) and a Site block in the inspector; `npm run worldmap -- --layer sites` and the drama check report peoples and sites.

## Phase 1 — World generation

- **M1.6 Travel and routes.** A travel-cost layer (biome, slope, river crossings, cheaper at fords and passes), a route graph joining harbours, fords, passes and fertile river mouths on each landmass plus sea lanes between harbours, and a likely-roads overlay (key R). The inspector gains a Travel row and a "Road" why line; the drama check prints nodes, edges and connectivity.
- **M1.5 Names and strategic features.** Ranges, rivers, forests, deserts, lakes, seas, islands and volcano clusters are detected and named from syllable tables, along with passes, fords, harbours, straits and bays. A Names overlay (key N) labels them by zoom, and the inspector lists a Places block.
- **M1.4 World map viewer and worker.** `/?next` opens on the world map. Generation runs in a Web Worker (`platform/worldgen-worker.ts`, `worldgen-host.ts`) with a stage-by-stage progress line, and the old map stays on screen, dimmed, until the new one arrives. `app/WorldMap.tsx` draws one base layer at a time from cached offscreen bitmaps, with pan, zoom (up to 8 px per cell, with a cell grid), a minimap and keyboard shortcuts. Rivers, lakes and sea and hillshade are overlays (new `water` render option). The legend has a value tick that follows the pointer, and a cell inspector gives "why" lines from the rule table in `content/map-why.ts`, each with a "show" link to its layer. Seed, size, layer, selected cell and zoom live in the URL (Generate pushes history; other changes replace it), and saves keep the generated map. The phone layout uses a layer chip row and a bottom-sheet inspector. The smoke test now covers generate → layer → inspect → save → reload → load.
- **Fix: rainfall bands and slope streaks.** Rainfall now sweeps all rows together with moisture mixing between neighbouring rows, blends the westerly and trade-wind sweeps across a wandering belt edge, and lets the rain belts wander too, so rainfall and biome borders no longer follow straight east-west lines. Plate-edge uplift is blurred, which removes the one-cell streaks that the 4-neighbour boundary distance carried down mountain slopes.
- **M1.3 Biomes, resources, aura, fertility and wildness.** `generateWorld(seed, params, content, onProgress?)` now takes the worldgen content and adds five stages (about 0.35 s in total at 256×256). Biomes come from a temperature × rainfall table in `content/biomes.ts` (mean temperature, with winter low gating jungle and savanna), plus alpine, swamp, coast, ocean and lake rules. Volcanoes sit inland of subducting oceanic plates. A faint aura layer has strong spots at peaks, river heads, old forests and hot springs (`auraSpots`, Phase 2 legendary-place candidates). Fifteen resources in `content/resources.ts` grow in seeded clusters with per-resource RNG streams and cell-share caps. Fertility (0–100) and wildness (0–100) layers feed Phase 2. New map layers (biome, fertility, wildness, aura, resources), drama columns and the `worldgen-check` project skill.
- **M1.2 Climate, water and erosion, plus worldgen tools.** `generateWorld` now runs terrain, erosion, climate and water stages (about 0.25 s at 256×256). Light stream-power erosion carves valleys; every cell gets distance to sea, a summer high and winter low (latitude 70°N to 10°S, lapse rate, wider swings inland), wind-swept rainfall with rain shadows, and drought, storm and snow risks. Priority flood gives every cell a downhill path to the sea, rivers above a flow threshold, lakes in larger depressions and floodplains. Cells at or below sea level are sea, including inland seas. New params `temperatureShift` and `rainfallScale`. Tools: `npm run drama` (world stats and stage timings), `npm run worldmap` (PNG per layer, Node `zlib`), layer palettes in `content/map-layers.ts`, a pure `renderLayer`, `checkWorldCells` and the `gridFromAscii` test helper.
- **M1.1 Terrain.** `generateWorld(seed, params)` builds a 256×256 heightmap in about 0.1 s: paired continental plates (inland ranges where they collide), oceanic plates (coastal ranges, island arcs, rifts), noise-warped plate borders, fractal coasts, ridged interior hills and an ocean margin. Sea level is set to hit `params.landFraction` (default 0.35); `params.continents` defaults to 4. Typed-array layers round-trip through saves. Added the `implementer` (Sonnet) and `test-runner` (Haiku) agents and the model-routing rules in `AGENTS.md`.

## Phase 0 — Reset and skeleton

- **M0.3 Engine review, sfc32 and saves.** The RNG is now `sfc32` (four 32-bit words per named stream, seeded with cyrb128 and checked against an independent reference vector). New `src/platform/save-store.ts` keeps named saves in IndexedDB with a SHA-256 checksum and one-transaction writes. `/?next` has Save, Load and Delete, and the Chromium smoke covers save → reload → load → continue.
- **M0.1–M0.2 Docs reset and slim CI.** Old docs, ADRs, probes, evidence, seed batteries, long-run tests, legacy browser specs and study scripts moved to `docs/archive/`. New short docs: vision, architecture, roadmap, decisions, content guide, deployment and the Phase 0 spec. CI is now one `core` job (format, types, boundaries, unit tests, build) and one Chromium smoke (`browser`). The boundary check rejects non-safe `Math` functions in `engine` and `content`.

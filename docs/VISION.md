# Vision

Story Generator is a breadth-first world simulator in the spirit of Dwarf Fortress, set in a **grounded medieval fantasy**. It generates a whole world with a few centuries of history, then lets you zoom into one place and watch it live day by day. The chronicle tells true stories of what the simulation did, in a historian's voice.

The full design is [REBOOT_PLAN.md](REBOOT_PLAN.md); this page is the short version.

## What you see

- A generated world: mountains, rivers, climates, biomes and **unevenly** spread resources that drive trade, envy, migration and war.
- Legends: civilizations, sites, families, notables, beasts, wars and ruins from about 250 years of fast-simulated history.
- A live site where people are born, grow up, love, quarrel, work, sicken, grieve, age and die, pushed around by seasons, weather, harvests, disease, fire, beasts, raiders and their own feuds.
- Later: competing kingdoms, institutions, deeper magic, and the choice to **steward** a settlement or **follow** one character.

## Pillars

1. **Simulation first.** Every chronicle line renders a real event record. Templates add flair, never facts.
2. **Ordinary life is the backbone.** Threats, politics and magic act *through* everyday life: hunger, grief, injury, marriage, migration, feuds.
3. **Breadth before depth.** Many simple, connected systems first; deepen them in later passes.
4. **Data over code.** Races, cultures, biomes, jobs, illnesses, spells, event rules and templates are content entries, so new things can be added without engine changes.
5. **Drama emerges.** No director, no quotas, no protected heroes. Tune only through content weights.
6. **Grounded scale.** Magic is rare and innate. Most people have none. Even a living legend, 0–3 alive at once, at most matches one low-end field army of about 1,000 soldiers.

## Never build

Cultivation or LitRPG stages, exponential power levels, "system" notifications, anyone stronger than one field army, a drama director, protected main characters, per-feature evidence documents or proof batteries, and frozen rule versions for old saves.
